Where this comes from

Every wall, door, window, balcony, lift and staircase is read from the vector drawings in the sanctioned building plan (RERA documents, file TS/069953/2024, dated 16-01-2025). Sheet 4 covers Aura, sheet 6 Bella and sheet 8 Clara, each drawn at 1:100. Floors 15, 24, 33, 42, 51 and the other special floors use their own drawings, so refuge areas sit where the approval puts them.

Tower, clubhouse and site-boundary positions come from the 1:200 site plan. Floor levels come from the approved sections: stilt at +0.60 m, 1st floor at +9.10 m, then 3.35 m per floor up to the terrace at +196.70 m.

Flat numbers, facing and saleable sizes come from the sales brochure. The courts, lawns and play areas are placed from the brochure master plan, to within about 1.5 m.

The surroundings come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL): the Outer Ring Road and its service roads, the Neopolis roads, Osman Sagar and Kokapet Lake, parks, and about 7,800 mapped buildings within 4.5 km. They are aligned to the site using the 36 m road on the west, the road on the north and Plot 8 on the east, so expect them to be within roughly 20–30 m. Where OpenStreetMap gives no height, a building's height is estimated from its size. Neighbouring high-rises still under construction (Brigade Gateway, My Home Nishada, Sattva Lakeridge, Prestige Clairemont, Casa Luxura, One by MSN, My Home Grava and My Home Apas) are drawn at their published tower count and floors, laid out approximately inside their mapped plots.

The selected flat is furnished in an Indian modern style with CC0 3D models and photo textures from Poly Haven: marble, teak, walnut, linen and velvet, plus brass, jaali, a jhoola and dhurrie rugs. Pieces are placed against the approved walls, clear of doors and windows. The furniture is illustrative, and other flats on the floor show simple blocks to keep the model fast.
