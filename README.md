# Rajapushpa Skyra in 3D

A walkable 3D model of Rajapushpa Skyra (Plot 7, Neopolis, Kokapet, Hyderabad). Find any flat by number, such as B-2904,
then walk through it or take the guided tour. A link like `#B-2904/tour` opens that flat straight away.

**How it was made**
- Walls, doors, windows, balconies, lifts and stairs are read from the sanctioned building plans in the project's RERA filing (TS/069953/2024).
- Flat numbers, facing and saleable sizes are from the sales brochure. Furniture is illustrative.
- The surroundings are from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL),
  aligned to the site to within roughly 20–30 m. Neighbouring towers under construction are drawn at their published floors, laid out approximately.
- 3D furniture models and photo textures are CC0 from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com).

This is an independent buyer's visualisation, not an official Rajapushpa Properties publication. Check the approved plans and your agreement for anything that matters.
